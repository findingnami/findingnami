iOS Apps / Readfully

Readfully

Track the books you want to read, are reading, and have read. No accounts, no ads, no feed. Your library stays on your iPhone.

Readfully is a quiet place to keep track of your reading. Add a book, update your progress, and see your year in books. That's it. No accounts, no ads, no social feed, and nothing to sign up for.

Keep your shelves

Sort your books onto three simple shelves: Want to Read, Reading, and Read. The book you're reading right now sits at the top of My Books, with a progress bar and a one-tap Update progress button.

Add books in seconds

Scan the barcode on the back of a book, or search by title, author, or ISBN. Readfully fills in the details and the cover for you. Can't find a book? Add it yourself, with your own cover from your photos if you like.

Track your progress

Update by page or by chapter, or both. Rate the book, keep a note, and edit your start and finish dates. Every update is kept in the book's activity, so you can look back at how your reading went.

See your year

Set a yearly goal and watch your progress toward it. Stats show books finished, pages in finished books, your average rating, your reading streak, and a books-per-month chart. Browse previous years with a tap.

Private by design

Your library is stored only on your iPhone. Readfully has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The only time anything leaves your phone is when you search for or scan a book: the title, author, or ISBN you looked up is sent to Open Library to find it.

Your data, your control

Back up your whole library to a file you keep yourself (Files, iCloud Drive, anywhere), and restore it on a new phone. Export your books as a spreadsheet any time. Delete everything with one tap if you want to start over.

Designed to be calm

A warm, paper-like design with hand-drawn art, clear type that grows with your text size, and full VoiceOver support. Readfully is made for one person: you and your books.
